AI Overview

A sourdough starter is a fermented mix of flour and water that captures wild yeast and bacteria. To make one, combine equal parts flour and water, then feed it daily for 5 to 7 days until it bubbles and doubles.

  • Use whole wheat or rye flour for a faster start
  • Keep it at 24–27 °C
  • Discard half before each feeding
