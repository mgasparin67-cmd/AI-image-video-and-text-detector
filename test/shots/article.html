Lisbon on a budget: 48 hours in the City of Seven Hills

By Sam Ortega · 6 min read

Lisbon is a vibrant tapestry of history, culture, and flavor that offers something for every traveler. From the winding streets of Alfama to the bustling waterfront of Cais do Sodré, the city serves as a testament to Portugal’s rich maritime heritage. Whether you’re savoring a warm pastel de nata or riding the iconic Tram 28, every moment invites you to slow down and embrace the city’s unique charm.

It’s important to note that exploring Lisbon doesn’t have to break the bank. Moreover, many of the city’s most memorable experiences are completely free, from sunset at the Miradouro da Senhora do Monte to wandering the colorful tiles of Bairro Alto. By planning ahead and embracing local traditions, you can unlock a truly unforgettable journey that showcases the very best of Portuguese hospitality.

Ultimately, Lisbon is more than just a destination; it’s an experience that lingers long after you leave. Its seamless blend of old-world charm and modern energy creates a dynamic landscape where every corner tells a story, fostering lasting memories and a deeper appreciation for the beauty of slow travel.
