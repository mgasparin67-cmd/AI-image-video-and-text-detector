Three days eating my way through Bogotá

Exploring Bogotá’s food scene is a truly unforgettable journey that offers something for everyone. From bustling markets to cozy family-run restaurants, the city’s culinary landscape serves as a vibrant testament to Colombia’s rich cultural heritage. Whether you’re savoring a steaming bowl of ajiaco or sampling fresh tropical fruits, each bite tells a story of tradition and innovation. It’s important to note that timing your visit can make all the difference, as many local favorites are busiest at lunchtime. Ultimately, Bogotá invites travelers to slow down, embrace new flavors, and create lasting memories.
